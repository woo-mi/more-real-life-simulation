Methodology and caveats

TERM
DEFINITION
Active user
A distinct non-internal user with at least one qualifying product event in the rolling window.
Windows
Dynamic rolling 7-day and 30-day windows, evaluated at report time.
Exclusions
Internal users, internal instances, and known background or infrastructure events.
Population
Records where instances.type is customer.
Roles
Admins and explorers resolve from role IDs in event properties.
Recency bands
The recency ratio is 7-day ÷ 30-day actives, banded on the same scale as every chart in this report:
Weak <40%
Typical 40–55%
Strong 55–70%
Exceptional >70%
